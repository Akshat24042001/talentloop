Before you start

  • This is a first-round interview conducted by an AI interviewer. It takes about minutes and has questions.
  • Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge, a working camera and microphone, in a quiet place. Headphones help.
  • Speak naturally and take your time. You can ask the interviewer to repeat a question.
  • You will be asked to share your entire screen for the whole interview.
  • Stay on this page in full screen. Leaving the page, switching windows, pasting text, other people on camera and similar events are recorded and reviewed by HR.
  • If your connection drops, rejoin within seconds using the Rejoin button. After that the interview closes.
