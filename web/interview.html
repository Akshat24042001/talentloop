Before you start

  • An AI interviewer talks with you, one question at a time. The question is always shown on your screen, and you can say "please repeat" any time.
  • Sit in a quiet, well-lit place. Use the latest Chrome or Edge. Headphones help.
  • Keep your camera on and your face visible. Only you should be in view.
  • Use one screen only. Disconnect any extra monitor before you start. A second screen is treated like leaving the interview.
  • You'll share your entire screen for the whole interview.
  • If your connection drops, rejoin within seconds using the Rejoin button.
