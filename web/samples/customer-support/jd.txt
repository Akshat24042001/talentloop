Customer Support Associate, Voice (0-2 years) - Mumbai (Office, rotational shifts)

About the role
You will handle inbound calls from broadband and mobile customers: billing questions, plan changes, outages and complaints. You own each call until the customer's issue is resolved or correctly escalated.

Must have
- Clear spoken English and Hindi
- Patience and empathy with upset customers
- Comfortable with a CRM and ticketing tool while talking
- Willing to work rotational shifts including weekends

Good to have
- Prior voice process or telecom support experience
- Typing speed of 30+ words per minute

Details
- Office at Andheri East, Mumbai; cab provided for night shifts
- Metrics: first call resolution, average handle time, CSAT
- Interview process: AI first round, voice and accent round, operations manager round
